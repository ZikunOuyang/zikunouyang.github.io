I am a third-year PhD student at Université Paris Dauphine and École normale supérieure, advised by [Guillaume Barraquand](https://www.phys.ens.fr/~barraquand/) and [Cristina Toninelli](https://www.ceremade.dauphine.fr/~toninelli/). Before starting my PhD, I completed my master's studies at Université Paris Dauphine and Université Paul Sabatier. I received my bachelor's degree from the University of Science and Technology of China.
 
My research interests lie in probability theory and statistical physics, with a particular focus on integrable probability and random matrix theory.
